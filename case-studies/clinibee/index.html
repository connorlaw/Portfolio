Case study

Bespoke Webflow site for a
clinical practice platform builder

Introduction

Clinibee, a well-known platform builder for clinical practices, had their sights set on transitioning away from a basic Squarespace site, when we were introduced by an ex-colleague of mine.

Their Squarespace site was lacking personality, and really served as more of a placeholder than an actual, useful resource for marketing or information.

They were already in the process of working with a talented web designer, so I was brought in to manage the transtion/develop the new site on a platform I'd recommend for this project - we eventually settled on Webflow, giving them loads of power under the hood but still remaining friendly to those on the team without technical knowledge.

Solution

After a handful of meetings to run through designs and branding, I was able to get started on setting up the Content Management System (CMS) on Webflow and laying the foundations of development on the platform.

Once this had been set up, the next step involved translating Clinibee's final designs into a fully functional website. There was a lot of bespoke work involved to ensure the vision was truly brought to life, meaning I had to rely on a good amount of my dev experience to get the job done.

As always, my focus was on ensuring the precision and quality of my work was as good as it gets; responsiveness was accounted for, as well as SEO implementation, performance enhacements, and much more.

Outcome

The new site has been a massive success for the team, finally giving them a site to be proud of sharing with their future customers.

The migration went extremely smoothly - so much so that, over the following years, would continually reach out for more work on the site, from new landing pages to a whole new CMS section for hosting articles.

I had a blast working with the team on this initial phase - as well as future projects we've since worked together on - and look forward to plenty more in the future.

Feel free to check out the Clinibee website, or take a look at another digital health project I worked on, MyEyeSite.
